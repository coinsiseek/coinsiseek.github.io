Amira: an AI influencer with one consistent identity

I created and ran Amira, an AI-generated Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu athlete on Instagram, in images and video, from January to March 2024. The hard part was never making one good image. It was making her the same person in every post: same face, same curls, same build, across new scenes, outfits and lighting.

In early 2024 there were no character reference sheets or identity-lock features to lean on. Consistency came from strict review: every output was compared against her established look, and anything that drifted was thrown out and regenerated.

How I would grade this image today

A posted image, reviewed against my own rubric. It works as a social post, but a vision data reviewer should flag it.

  1. Text rendering, fail. The gi lettering reads as nonsense ("MOTX").
  2. Text rendering, fail. The sleeve and chest patches are illegible pseudo-logos.
  3. Background text, fail. The wall banners are unreadable smudges.
  4. Identity, minor drift. Curls are tighter and lighter than her established look.

Verdict: FIX. Anatomy, hands and belt are correct, and the sport details are plausible.

How I judge an AI image or video

The checklist I apply to generated visuals, written the way I would hand it to a team of reviewers.

CriterionWhat I checkTypical failure
Prompt adherenceEvery requested subject, action, style and constraint is presentMissing object, wrong count, ignored style
AnatomyHands, fingers, joints, proportions, contact between bodiesSix fingers, merged limbs, impossible grips
Identity consistencySame character stays the same person across images or framesFace drifts, age or build changes between shots
Text renderingSigns, logos and labels are spelled correctly and legibleGarbled pseudo-letters
Physics & lightingShadows, reflections and light direction agree; objects rest and move plausiblyTwo light sources that can't coexist, floating objects
Temporal stability (video)Objects keep their shape and position from frame to frameFlicker, morphing, limbs popping in and out
ArtifactsEdges, textures and backgrounds are clean at full sizeSmearing, repeated patterns, warped backgrounds
Safety & policyReal people, minors, explicit or harmful content, brand marksLikeness of a real person, unsafe content
PASSmeets every criterion FIXone minor issue, can be corrected in edit REJECTany anatomy, identity, safety or prompt failure
